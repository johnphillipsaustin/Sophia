Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming abandoned and neglected animals across the region. We believe in compassionate care, community education, and responsible pet ownership.

Our History & Impact

Founded by animal welfare advocates, our shelter has successfully placed thousands of pets into loving homes over the years. Through our network of foster parents and volunteers, we provide medical care, training, and rehabilitation for animals with special needs.
